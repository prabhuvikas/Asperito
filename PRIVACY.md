# FrameGuide Privacy

FrameGuide requests camera access to show a live preview and capture photographs. It requests add-only access to the Photos library when the user saves a photo. FrameGuide does not upload photos, collect analytics, require an account, or contact external services.

Preferences, tutorial choices, and overlay settings are saved locally through UserDefaults/AppStorage. iOS may manage app data according to system backup policies. The developer must verify all privacy declarations and App Store disclosure questions before publication.
