# Asperito Privacy

Asperito requests camera access to show a live preview and capture photographs. It requests add-only access to the Photos library when the user saves a photo. The Horizon Level guide uses the iPhone motion sensors to display alignment; this motion information is processed on-device and is not saved or transmitted. Asperito does not upload photos, collect analytics, require an account, or contact external services.

Preferences, tutorial choices, and overlay settings are saved locally through UserDefaults/AppStorage. iOS may manage app data according to system backup policies. The developer must verify all privacy declarations and App Store disclosure questions before publication.
